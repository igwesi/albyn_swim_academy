About Us

Since 2018, Albyn Swim Academy Ltd has provided swimming lessons for learners of all ages throughout Abuja and Nigeria. Offering year-round lessons, our family-run company operates the only indoor heated swim academy in the country. Our mission is to raise awareness of water safety and save lives. We provide male and female mixed sessions and also female-only sessions if you prefer.

Affiliations

All our tutors are professionally trained and accredited by Starfish Aquatics Institute (SAI) and the International Federation of Swim Teachers Association (IFSTA). Albyn Swim Academy Ltd is a proud member of the Aquatic Federation of Nigeria.
