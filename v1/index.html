About Us

Since 2018, Albyn Swim Academy Ltd has provided swimming lessons for learners of all ages throughout Abuja and Nigeria. Offering year-round lessons, our family-run company operates the only indoor heated swim academy in the country. Backed by many years of experience and professionally accredited instructors, our mission is to raise awareness of water safety and save lives. We provide male and female mixed sessions and also female-only sessions if you prefer.
