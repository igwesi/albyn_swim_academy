Campaigns

Help Us Save Lives

As part of our initiative to raise awareness of the benefits of learning to swim, Albyn Swim Academy Ltd is running several campaigns to reach out to communities in Abuja and the surrounding areas in Nigeria. Together, we can prevent drowning incidents and save lives. Get in touch today to find out how you can help us achieve our mission.

Free Swim Lessons for Children in Welfare Homes

Working closely with orphanages and welfare homes, we offer free swimming classes to young people so that they can learn how to swim and be safe in the water.

Sponsor a Child to Swim

Children are the heart of our academy and there are many children from lower-class and less-privileged homes that do not have the resources to learn to swim. That’s why we are partnering with corporate organizations to join our initiative and sponsor a child to learn to swim by paying for their swimming lessons at our academy.

Want to Get Involved?

In addition to swimming lessons, our school campaigns for greater awareness of water safety.
